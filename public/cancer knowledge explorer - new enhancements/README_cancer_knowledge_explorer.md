## 🤔 COMMON QUESTIONS

### Q: Do I need to read all 7 documents?
**A:** No. Pick your role (Project Manager / Developer / Content / Medical) and follow that path (2-3 documents, ~1 hour).

### Q: Can we just do disclaimers first?
**A:** Yes. Phase 1 (disclaimers only) is ~1 week and costs ~$3-5K. See Executive Summary, Budget section.

### Q: Who should be on the project team?
**A:** Minimum: 1 React dev, 1 content reviewer, 1 legal contact. Optional but recommended: 1 oncologist/MD for fact-checking.

### Q: What's the highest-risk item?
**A:** Outdated medical information. The Audit Checklist and Accuracy Template directly address this.

### Q: Can we use AI to help with content audit?
**A:** Yes. The Implementation Guide includes sample code for using Claude API to assist with fact-checking.

### Q: What's the simplest first step?
**A:** Add disclaimers (Week 1). Doesn't require source validation or medical review. Immediately reduces legal risk.

### Q: How long does a full implementation take?
**A:** 6-8 weeks with a team of 3-4 (dev, content, medical). Pilot (disclaimers only) is 1-2 weeks.
