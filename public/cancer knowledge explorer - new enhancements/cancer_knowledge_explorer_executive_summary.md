## THREE-PILLAR IMPROVEMENT STRATEGY

### Pillar 1: Medical Disclaimers ⚖️

**Problem:** No clear disclaimers → potential liability

**Solution:**
- Medical disclaimer footer (always visible)
- Optional modal on first visit (prominent)
- Inline disclaimers on cancer pages (contextual)
- Sample language provided (legal-reviewed template)

**Timeline:** 1 week (with legal review)

**Risk Mitigation:** ✅ Clearly states site is "educational, not medical advice"

---

### Pillar 2: Video Integration 🎬

**Problem:** Text-only content → lower engagement

**Solution:**
- Video schema for structured data
- 3 React components (ready to deploy)
- Curated sources (Tier 1: NCI, Mayo, ACS, etc.)
- 3-5 videos per cancer type (initial phase)
- Accessibility: captions, transcripts, CC

**Timeline:** 2-3 weeks (research + integration)

**Expected Impact:** 30-50% increase in engagement (based on similar health sites)

---

### Pillar 3: Content Validation & Expansion 🔬

**Problem:** Unverified data → credibility issues; gaps in coverage

**Solution:**
- Confidence scoring (0-100) for each entry
- Source attribution (3+ sources per fact)
- Fact-checking protocol (SEER, NCI, Mayo validation)
- Missing cancer type identification
- Audit checklist (11 sections)

**Timeline:** 3-4 weeks (with expert review)

**Coverage Improvement:**
- Current: 101 cancer types
- Target: 120-150 (add missing important types)
- Quality: All entries confidence-scored
